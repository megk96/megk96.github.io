About

Architecture before abstraction.

Senior Applied AI Engineer designing foundational AI systems: platform primitives, reasoning infrastructure, evaluation frameworks, and production-grade architecture for complex decisions.

Background

I design AI systems that can survive contact with production: clear boundaries, reusable primitives, observable decisions, explicit human control, and evaluation that shapes the system rather than merely scoring it.

My work centers on the architecture surrounding models — how responsibilities are defined, how context is governed, how handoffs carry structured meaning, and how human judgment is designed into the control path rather than appended at the end.

Focus areas

  • AI platform architecture and reusable platform primitives
  • Evaluation infrastructure as an architectural layer
  • Reasoning infrastructure and long-running AI workflows
  • Production-grade architecture for complex, high-stakes decisions
